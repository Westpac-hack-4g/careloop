Financial hardship (8): Once we are aware of your financial difficulty we will suspend collections activity for 21 days, while we are assessing your situation, as per legislative requirements. This will enable you to provide us with any additional information if applicable. If we agree to provide you with hardship assistance and you comply with the terms of your arrangement, there will be no collections activity during any approved assistance period. Can someone else negotiate on my behalf? Yes, with your signed approval we can discuss and negotiate a solution with your nominated representative such as a Financial Counsellor or other third party.
