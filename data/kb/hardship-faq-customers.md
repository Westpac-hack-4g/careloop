Frequently Asked Questions: How long is the assistance period? We understand each person and situation is unique. We will always try to help provide a tailored solution to suit your individual needs. This might be for a short, medium or long period of time depending on your circumstances. What happens if my circumstances don't change at the end of the assistance period? If this is the case, please contact the Assist team who will re-assess your situation and may recommend you seek financial advise through a free service What happens when I submit the online form?

Frequently Asked Questions: Once you have submitted your financial hardship application, we will send you a confirmation email with your application ID. A decision on your application will be provided to you within 21 days. If we need to discuss your application, we will aim to contact you within 5 business days. What kind of supporting documents do I need? Solutions are assessed on a case by case basis and you may need to provide some further information such as Centrelink documents, medical information, employment status, proof of income, liabilities and expenditure. If required our Consultants will discuss these requirements with you. What type of assistance is available?

Frequently Asked Questions: Assistance could include flexible payment arrangements or variations to your loan repayments. We are committed to working with you to provide a solution that will better your financial position. How does a hardship arrangement affect my credit report? A financial hardship arrangement may help improve your repayment information on your credit report, if you meet your revised repayments under your hardship arrangement. Find out more (PDF 1MB) Will I still be contacted by Collections whilst assistance is in place? No, there will be no collections activity during any approved assistance period when you comply with the terms of your arrangement. Can someone else negotiate on my behalf?

Frequently Asked Questions: Yes, we're happy to talk to a relative, friend or financial counselling organisation about your accounts. We'll just need your authorisation first to maintain your privacy What happens if the bank doesn't support my request for financial hardship? We will advise you in writing of the reason. You may wish to seek further financial advice from a financial counsellor or other third party, about the options available to you. What happens if I am not happy with your response?

Frequently Asked Questions: If you are not satisfied with our response or handling of your complaint, you may be able to lodge your complaint with the free, independent external dispute resolution scheme, the Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA). The contact details for AFCA are set out below.
