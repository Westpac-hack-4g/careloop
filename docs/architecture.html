CareLoop System Architecture

Draft for the team · 16 Nov 2026 · proposals are marked as such

CareLoop is three services with two hand-offs between them. Risk Tracking works out who is heading for trouble, CareLoop talks to that customer and gets a request to a person, and Risk Reporting tells Westpac staff what is coming, by area, without names.

Where one service ends and the next begins

What each service owns

ServiceIts jobWho uses the AI, and for whatIn the repo today
1 · Risk Tracking For every opted-in customer across all of Westpac: read the transactions, work out the tier and any shortfall, keep a daily history. AI sorts transactions and flags unusual patterns. Rules do every calculation. A stand-in: lib/tier/ plus pre-sorted sample data. No AI in it yet.
2 · CareLoop Everything the customer and the Assist team touch: heads-up, chat, request, receipt and deadline, staff console, decision, check-in. AI answers from Westpac's pages, turns the customer's words into form fields and flags sensitive situations. It never calculates or decides. Built: lib/conversation/, lib/ai/, lib/rag/, components/.
3 · Risk Reporting Turn the daily tiers and the outcomes into counts and trends for staff. No names leave this service. None needed for counting. AI could write the monthly commentary later. Nothing.

When each part runs

Reporting runs on a clock, straight after the nightly risk run. It is never set off by one customer's action. The area view refreshes every day because staff act on it this week; the portfolio report is monthly because it is about trends.

Reports by branch: group by area, leave the names out

Why grouping by area is right

  • Analysis covers all of Westpac, but trouble is local: a large employer cuts shifts, a flood hits one town. It shows up in one area first.
  • An area manager can act on "14 customers here are likely to fall short in the next 30 days, up from 6": more appointment slots, a local outreach day, an early word to the Assist team.
  • The same daily data rolls up into the monthly portfolio report, so there is one source and two views.

Why the area view should not list customers

  • The customer agreed to an early heads-up for themselves. They did not agree to appear on a list that branch staff read.
  • Our own workflow already says "totals only, no names" for the report. A small branch needs one more rule: hide any group under 10 so nobody can be worked out.
  • Named follow-up already has a route. A customer who starts a request, says they cannot pay, or asks for a person goes to Westpac Assist through CareLoop.

To settle as a team

  • Who builds Risk Tracking, and does it arrive as a separate server or as a module in the same repository? That decides how hand-off A is wired.
  • The exact fields of the risk event (A). CareLoop already uses: tier, reasons, shortfall and date, the 30-day forecast, and the dated timeline.
  • Whether the forecast comes with the risk event or on request (B). On request keeps account detail out of CareLoop until the customer allows it.
  • Whether Westpac branches look after a set of customers at all. Hardship is handled centrally by Westpac Assist, so "region" may be the more realistic grouping than "branch". Worth asking a mentor.
  • Whether Risk Reporting stays a roadmap slide or gets one screen in the demo.
