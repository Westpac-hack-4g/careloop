Bear in mind, whenever you repay less , you may end up paying more interest over the life of your loan. Will a financial hardship arrangement affect my credit report? A financial hardship arrangement will appear on your credit report, but it should not impact your credit score - as long as you meet your revised repayments under the arrangement.

What else can you do?: The reasons for your financial hardship arrangement will not be included in the credit report. And financial hardship information only stays on a credit report for 12 months: after 12 months the fact that you entered into a hardship arrangement will no longer be visible on your credit report.
